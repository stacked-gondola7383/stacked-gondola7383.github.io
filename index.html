⚙️ Understanding Data Paths and Config

A "data path" is simply the address of a folder on your computer. For example, a path might look like a chain of folder names separated by slashes. Games use data paths to know where to read and write their files.

WWE 2K25 keeps several kinds of files in these locations:

  • Configuration files — these record your preferences and the settings the game reads at startup. They are usually small text files.
  • Cache files — temporary data the game creates to speed things up. Caches rebuild themselves when needed, which is why they are safe to clear when they get large.
  • Export folders — the destination folders where content you export from the game is written.

The helper surfaces all three so you can see exactly what exists on your machine, open a folder with one click, and understand what each one is for. Nothing is changed unless you choose to change it.

📁 Export Caches and Export Folders

Export caches are the part most people never think about until something goes wrong. Over time, they can grow large, or they can hold stale entries from an older version of the game. When that happens, exports may behave oddly or take longer than they should.

With wwe-2k25-desktop you can:

  • See where the export cache lives on your system.
  • Check how much space it is using.
  • Open the export folder directly so you can find the files you created.
  • Clean up cache data when you want a fresh start.

If you are the kind of player who exports a lot of content, having these folders one click away saves a surprising amount of time.
